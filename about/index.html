About Express Cargo

Smart logistics, even when it gets complex.

Since 2002 we have delivered high-quality logistics for shipments that need a little more attention and expertise, from dangerous goods to high-end fashion, works of art and aircraft parts.

Since 2002

Fast and safe, to any destination.

Express Cargo was founded in 2002 to provide fast and safe freight transport to every destination in the world. Through our broad network of agents we offer a complete air, sea and road service, and the experience and skills of our team guarantee quality and accuracy.

Our specialisation is dangerous goods, clinical trials and sample shipments on dry ice. As the Dutch office of the Express-Cargo Group, we are based at Europe’s key cargo airport: Amsterdam Schiphol.

  • Dangerous goods and temperature-sensitive shipments
  • Luxury goods, art and aircraft parts
  • Tailor-made packaging and handling, with care for every detail
  • Door to door, including remote areas

Our strength is people, not process.

Whether it is a sample on dry ice, a fragile artwork or a (model) car: we arrange it.
