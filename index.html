A partner you can rely on.

Express Cargo was founded in 2002 to provide fast and safe cargo transport to any destination in the world. As part of the Express-Cargo Group, with offices at Amsterdam Schiphol, Europe's key cargo airport, and in Serbia, we offer international and domestic freight, forwarding, customs brokerage and warehousing.

Every shipment is managed by a dedicated coordinator from start to finish. You have one direct point of contact, fast communication, and a transport solution tailored to your schedule, cargo and customs requirements.
