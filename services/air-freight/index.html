How an air shipment moves.

5 steps · one coordinator

PICKUP DOCS AMS ARR POD
  1. 01 / 05 · PICKUP

    Collection

    We collect at your door, or at your supplier anywhere in our network of local agents.

    • Pickup from your door or your supplier
    • Packed and labelled for the route
    • Collection confirmed by your coordinator
  2. 02 / 05 · DOCS

    Documents & DGR check

    Export customs filed, dangerous goods declared and packed to IATA DGR. Every document in order before the cargo moves.

    • Air waybill and commercial invoice
    • Shipper’s Declaration for Dangerous Goods when needed
    • Export declaration filed with customs
  3. 03 / 05 · AMS

    Uplift at Schiphol

    Booked on the flight that fits the cargo and the deadline, built up and loaded.

    • Booked on the flight that fits cargo and deadline
    • Built up and loaded at Schiphol
    • Flight details sent to you
  4. 04 / 05 · ARR

    Arrival & clearance

    Our agent at destination handles import formalities, so the shipment does not wait in a shed.

    • Received by our agent at destination
    • Import clearance and duties handled
    • Temperature and DG requirements checked again
  5. 05 / 05 · POD

    Delivered

    Signed for at the door. You get the proof of delivery and one coordinator answered every question on the way.

    • Delivered to the named address
    • Proof of delivery shared with you
    • One coordinator from start to finish

What we fly.

Shipments that need more than a booking: the right packing, the right paperwork and someone who follows up.

  • Dangerous goods

    Declared, packed and labelled to IATA DGR by certified staff.

  • Lithium batteries

    UN3480, UN3481 and UN3090/3091, in or with equipment.

  • Biological & pharmaceutical

    Clinical samples, UN3373 specimens and pharma, on dry ice when needed.

  • Temperature-controlled

    +2–8 °C, +15–25 °C and frozen, from packaging to active containers.

  • Customs documents

    Export and import formalities handled, documents prepared and checked.

  • Bonded warehousing

    Storage under customs control at Schiphol until the goods move on.
