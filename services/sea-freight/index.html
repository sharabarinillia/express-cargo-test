How a sea shipment moves.

5 steps · one coordinator

PICKUP STUFF POL OCEAN POD
  1. 01 / 05 · PICKUP

    Collection

    Collected at the supplier, by low-loader if it has to be, and brought to the port.

    • Collected at the supplier
    • Low-loader for heavy or oversize cargo
    • Brought to the port or the consolidation warehouse
  2. 02 / 05 · STUFF

    Into the container

    Your own container (FCL), or consolidated with other cargo in one container (LCL). Sealed, secured, documented.

    • FCL: your own 20′, 40′ or 40′ high cube
    • LCL: consolidated with other cargo
    • Sealed, secured and documented
  3. 03 / 05 · POL

    Port of loading

    Export customs cleared and loaded on the carrier that fits the route and the date.

    • Export customs cleared
    • Bill of lading issued
    • Loaded on the carrier that fits route and date
  4. 04 / 05 · OCEAN

    At sea

    We follow the vessel and tell you when anything changes, not after.

    • The vessel followed throughout
    • Changes reported as they happen
    • Arrival notice prepared
  5. 05 / 05 · POD

    Port to door

    Our partner at destination clears the cargo and delivers it to your door.

    • Import clearance at destination
    • Container delivered or unloaded
    • Delivered to your door
