    The rules behind the numbers.

    Volumetric conversion factors by mode
    ModeVolumetric ruleIn practice
    Air freightL × W × H (cm) ÷ 6,0001 m³ = 166.67 kg. Chargeable weight rounds up to the next 0.5 kg.
    Courier & expressL × W × H (cm) ÷ 5,0001 m³ = 200 kg. Integrators set their own divisor; 5,000 is the common one.
    Road (Europe)1 m³ = 333 kg · 1 LDM = 1,750 kgGroupage is charged on the greatest of weight, volume and loading metres.
    Sea LCL1 m³ = 1,000 kg (W/M)Charged per revenue tonne: whichever is greater, cubic metres or tonnes.

    Indicative only. Carriers and routes can apply their own factors and minimums, and dangerous goods or temperature control can change the packing. Your coordinator confirms the final chargeable weight in the quote.
